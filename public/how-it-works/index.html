How PricePulse World works

PricePulse World is designed to help shoppers discover community-reported prices and contribute useful updates from shops they visit.

1. Explore available information

Open the app and browse its search and shop sections. Listings depend on information that has actually been submitted to the platform. If there are no approved reports for an item or area yet, there may be nothing to compare.

2. Submit a price you really saw

Create an account or sign in, then submit the exact product name, shop name, location, price, currency, and date you saw it. Please do not guess prices or submit information you cannot reasonably confirm.

3. Understand report status

  • Pending verification: submitted, but not yet approved.
  • Approved: reviewed and approved by a reviewer.
  • Rejected: not accepted as a trusted listing.
Important: An approved report reflects a review decision, not a guarantee that the shop still charges the same amount. Prices and stock can change. Contact the shop to confirm before travelling or purchasing.

4. Help keep information useful

Submit accurate, recent observations. Include enough location detail to identify the shop, and avoid duplicate or misleading reports.

Frequently asked questions

How do I submit a product price?

Sign in and share the product, shop, location, price you observed, currency, and date. Only submit prices you actually saw.

What does pending verification mean?

It means a report has been submitted but has not yet been approved. Check its status before treating it as verified.

Are approved prices always current?

No. Prices and stock can change. Check the report date and confirm the current price with the shop.

Does PricePulse World cover every shop in Nigeria?

No. Available coverage depends on real community submissions. The platform starts with a focus on Lagos and can grow as people contribute accurate reports.
