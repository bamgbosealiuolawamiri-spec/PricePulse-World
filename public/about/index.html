PricePulse World

Helping shoppers make more informed price decisions

PricePulse World helps people share and explore product prices reported at real shops. The goal is simple: make it easier to check prices, compare available information, and make more informed shopping choices.

Built around community contributions

People can submit a price they actually saw, including the product, shop, location, currency, and date. New submissions remain pending until reviewed.

Accuracy matters

Community reports can change over time. A submitted price is not automatically a verified price, and a listed price may no longer match what a shop charges today. Check the date and status, and confirm important prices with the shop before buying.

Starting locally, designed to grow

PricePulse World starts with a focus on helping shoppers in Lagos, Nigeria, while supporting reports from other places and currencies.
